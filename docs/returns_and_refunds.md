# Returns and refunds

## Missing or damaged items

If an item is missing, damaged or spoiled, report it in the Orders tab within 48 hours of delivery. Include a photo of any damaged or spoiled item. Reports made after 48 hours cannot be accepted.

## How refunds are paid

You can choose how to receive a refund. A refund to your original payment method takes 5 to 7 business days to appear. A refund as Kikapu credit is added to your account instantly. M-Pesa refunds are usually received within 24 hours.

## Returning items

Unopened non-perishable items can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Request a return in the Orders tab and a driver will collect the item on your next delivery at no charge.

Perishable items, such as fresh produce, meat, dairy and bakery items, cannot be returned. If a perishable item arrives damaged or spoiled, report it within 48 hours instead and it will be refunded.

## Refunds for cancelled orders

If you cancel an order while its status is Received, you receive a full refund, including the delivery fee.
