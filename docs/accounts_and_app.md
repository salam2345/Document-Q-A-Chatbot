# Accounts and the app

## Creating an account

You can sign up with an email address or a mobile phone number. Each phone number can be linked to only one Kikapu account.

## Resetting your password

Tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen and we will send a reset link to your email or phone. The reset link expires after 30 minutes. If it expires, request a new one.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings > Privacy > Delete account. Deletion is processed within 30 days. Any unused Kikapu credit is lost when the account is deleted, and order history cannot be recovered.

## Kikapu Plus

Kikapu Plus is an optional membership that costs $9.99 per month in Canada or KES 499 per month in Kenya. Members get free delivery on smaller orders (see the Delivery page) and earn 5% of every order back as Kikapu credit. New members get a 14-day free trial. You can cancel Kikapu Plus at any time in Settings > Membership, and your benefits continue until the end of the current billing month.

## Contacting support

Support is available through in-app chat every day from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. local time. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next morning.

## Supported devices

The Kikapu app works on iPhones running iOS 15 or later and on Android phones running Android 9 or later.
