# Orders and payments

## Minimum order

The minimum order is $15 in Kamloops and KES 500 in Nairobi.

## Payment methods

In Canada, Kikapu accepts Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and Google Pay. In Kenya, Kikapu accepts M-Pesa, Visa and Mastercard. Cash on delivery is not accepted in either country. Kikapu credit is always applied to your order total before your card or M-Pesa is charged.

## Changing or cancelling an order

You can edit or cancel an order for free while its status is Received. Once the status changes to Packing, the order can no longer be edited or cancelled in the app, but you can still contact support through in-app chat for help.

## Substitutions

If an item is out of stock, our shoppers follow your substitution preference. You can choose "Replace with a similar item", "Replace with a specific item" or "Don't replace, refund me" for each product in your cart. If a substitute costs more, you are never charged more than the price of the original item. If it costs less, you pay the lower price.

## Promo codes

You can use one promo code per order. Promo codes cannot be combined with each other, and most expire 30 days after they are issued. Enter a promo code on the checkout screen before you place the order; codes cannot be added after an order is placed.
