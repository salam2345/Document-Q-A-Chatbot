# Delivery

## Delivery areas

Kikapu currently delivers in two cities: Kamloops, British Columbia (Canada) and Nairobi (Kenya). Orders to addresses outside these two cities cannot be placed in the app. We plan to announce new cities in the app's News tab before they launch.

## Delivery fees

In Kamloops, the delivery fee is $4.99 per order. Delivery is free on orders of $50 or more before tax.

In Nairobi, the delivery fee is KES 150 per order. Delivery is free on orders of KES 3,000 or more.

Kikapu Plus members get free delivery on orders of $25 or more in Kamloops and KES 1,500 or more in Nairobi.

## Delivery windows

Deliveries run every day from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. local time. When you check out, you choose a 2-hour delivery window.

Orders placed before 2:00 p.m. can be delivered the same day, as long as a window is still available. Orders placed after 2:00 p.m. are delivered the next day at the earliest.

## Tracking your order

You can follow your order in the Orders tab. The status moves through Received, Packing, Out for Delivery and Delivered. When your order is Out for Delivery, you can see the driver's location on a live map.

## Missed deliveries

If nobody is available to receive the order, the driver waits for 10 minutes and tries to call you. After that, the order is returned to the store. You can reschedule a missed delivery from the Orders tab for a rescheduling fee of $3 in Kamloops or KES 100 in Nairobi. Frozen and refrigerated items from a missed delivery cannot be redelivered and are refunded as Kikapu credit.
